# Aakhri Local — Episode 5: "Shukriya" (Finale)
**Runtime:** 60s · **Format:** 9:16 vertical (master) + 16:9 YouTube · 60fps · **Language:** Hindi
**Date in story:** 14 July 2026 (Sudha's 51st birthday) · **Time:** 1:11 am → dawn, then an epilogue some days later

## Logline
51 years to the minute, Meera brings Sudha to the last local. The man who saved her finally hears thank you, says his name, and goes home.

## Cast in this episode
- **Meera**: the grey cardigan over a plain white kurta, the braid, the watch (stopped at 1:12 since Ep1).
- **Sudha**: a white cotton saree with a thin maroon border, a shawl, the tin box held to her chest.
- **Raghunath**: his usual look; tonight the tiffin is on his lap and he sits **in seat 0, by the open door**.

## Music plan
- **0:00–0:07** A quiet tanpura drone, rain, the station hum.
- **0:07–0:19** The old man's warm theme (bansuri) returns, slow and full of emotion.
- **0:19–0:32** Full strings join. The emotional peak, held back a little.
- **0:32–0:41** Down to piano + bansuri.
- **0:41–0:49** The tunnel exit: a big swell, choir "aah", bells. Dawn.
- **0:49–1:00** A gentle resolution: a solo piano version of the series theme. The watch ticks in time with the final notes.
