# Aakhri Local — Episode 2: "Seat 0"
**Runtime:** 60s · **Format:** 9:16 vertical (master) + 16:9 YouTube · 60fps · **Language:** Hindi
**Date in story:** the next night · **Time:** 1:10 am → 1:12 am

## Logline
Meera takes the last local on purpose. He's waiting. Over a warm, funny conversation he tells her everything except his name, and then he leaves a tiffin behind.

## Cast in this episode
- **Meera**: same outfit, braid dry tonight, dark circles. The ticket is in her cardigan pocket.
- **The old man**: same, plus a small round steel tiffin (2 tiers) on his lap.

## Music plan
- **0:00–0:07** A low string pad: anticipation, unease.
- **0:07–0:32** The warm theme: bansuri (flute) melody over soft piano. Their friendship.
- **0:32–0:41** The flute drops out. Piano only, slower.
- **0:41–0:47** Silence + clock tick. A hard cut to black on the flicker.
- **0:47–1:00** A low drone. A soft bell (like a temple bell) on the tiffin reveal. A reverse swell on "Part 3 →".
