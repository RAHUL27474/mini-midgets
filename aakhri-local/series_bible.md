# Aakhri Local — series bible
**Format:** 5 episodes × 60s · 9:16 Shorts/Reels + 16:9 YouTube · 60fps · Hindi
**Genre:** mystery with heart. Each episode ends on a hook, and the finale is emotional, not horror.

## Logline
A night-shift nurse keeps meeting the same old man on Mumbai's last local train. He hands her a ticket from 1975, and it leads her to the stranger who saved her mother's life.

## Backstory (the truth, revealed slowly)
- On 14 July 1975 a fire broke out at 1:12 am in the last local from VT to Thane. Raghunath Joshi, a 68-year-old retired schoolteacher going home to his granddaughter, carried a stranger's 6-month-old baby out of the burning coach. He went back in for her mother and never came out. His body was never identified. Only half a burnt ticket was found: seat 0, his joke for "the seat nobody wants, next to the door."
- The baby was **Sudha**, Meera's mother. Sudha's mother survived with burns and always told her "ek anjaan farishte ne bachaya." The family celebrates 14 July as Sudha's birthday, the day she was saved. She keeps the half-burnt ticket found on her rescuer, and her baby shawl, in a biscuit tin.
- Every year on 14 July, at 1:12 am, Raghunath rides the last local. He has been waiting for someone to remember him.

## World and visual rules
- **The 1:12 am local:** an almost empty second-class coach, flickering tube lights, green seats, rain on the windows, city lights streaking past.
- Raghunath is always lit slightly warmer than everything else. That's the visual clue. In Ep5 the warm light fills the whole coach.
- **Recurring props:** the yellowed ticket "14-07-1975 · VT → THANE · SEAT 0", Meera's cracked watch (stops at 1:12 whenever he is near), the leather satchel, a small steel tiffin he carries for his granddaughter.
- **Palette:** cold blue-green night. Warm amber only around Raghunath. Sepia for 1975 flashbacks. The finale goes to dawn gold.
