# Aakhri Local — Episode 4: "Sudha"
**Runtime:** 60s · **Format:** 9:16 vertical (master) + 16:9 YouTube · 60fps · **Language:** Hindi
**Date in story:** 13 July, evening · **Time:** about 8 pm

## Logline
Meera brings the newspaper to her mother. Sudha has kept the other half of the ticket for 51 years and never knew whose it was.

## Cast in this episode
- **Meera**: the same mustard kurta, damp from the rain, holding the folded newspaper.
- **Sudha** (50): a cotton saree (pale green with a maroon border), grey-streaked hair in a bun, reading glasses on a chain. Small, gentle, practical.

## Locations
- An old chawl-style building: wooden stairs, a corridor with clotheslines, one lit window.
- Sudha's one-room flat: a small kitchen corner, a steel utensil rack, a wall calendar, a framed photo of her late mother (Meera's Nani) with a marigold garland, a window with rain and a view of the railway line.

## Music plan
- **0:00–0:07** Rain + home sounds (a pressure cooker, a TV murmuring next door). A soft acoustic guitar.
- **0:07–0:18** The guitar stops. Silence. Just the rain.
- **0:18–0:33** Sudha's theme: sarangi over a low string pad, mournful.
- **0:33–0:44** The ticket theme from Ep1 (piano) returns and joins the sarangi.
- **0:44–0:56** Strings swell and resolve.
- **0:56–1:00** A train horn in the distance becomes the music; reverse swell on "Finale →".
