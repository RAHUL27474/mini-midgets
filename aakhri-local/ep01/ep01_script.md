## Logline
After a 14-hour ICU shift, Meera catches the last local. A gentle old man returns a ticket she never dropped. It's dated 1975, and the coach was empty all along.

## Cast in this episode
- **Meera**: scrubs, grey cardigan, tote bag, cracked silver watch, wet braid.
- **The old man (Raghunath, unnamed here)**: sweater vest, kurta, round glasses, satchel. Always warmly lit.
- **TC**: black coat, ticket punch, umbrella under his arm.

## Music plan
- **0:00–0:11** "Night Shift": a lone piano in a minor key, with rain ambience underneath.
- **0:11–0:28** Piano + soft tanpura drone. Warm and a little nostalgic when the old man appears.
- **0:28–0:42** The drone thins. A high violin note is held, and tension creeps in.
- **0:42–0:55** Music drops out. Only the train's rhythm and rain.
- **0:55–1:00** One deep piano chord + a reverse-cymbal swell on "Part 2 →".
