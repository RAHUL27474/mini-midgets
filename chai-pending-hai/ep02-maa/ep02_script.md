# Chai Pending Hai — Episode 2: "Maa"
**Runtime:** 60s · **Format:** 9:16 vertical + 16:9 YouTube · **Language:** Hindi

Picks up seconds after Ep1: Kabir has just read the clipping and learned the birthday messages come from Sameer's house. The "Seen" on his message was Sameer's mother, Shanta Devi.

## Story
Kabir rides through the wet night city to Sameer's old house. Shanta Devi opens the door holding Sameer's cracked phone. She has been sending the messages: Sameer had set a yearly reminder on his phone, "Kabir ka birthday — chai pending hai", and every year when it rings she finishes what her son started. Kabir breaks down on the doorstep; she sits beside him. In the kitchen two glasses of chai are already poured. At dawn, two glasses steam on the step with Billu the cat between them, and Kabir finally pays for the chai. Then Sameer's phone lights up with a message from an unknown number: "Kabir pahunch gaya? — S". Part 3.
